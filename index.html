Privacy at a glance

Plain-language summary — the full policy has the details.

  1. 1
    Location is used on your device Coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo only to fetch a forecast. They are not stored on our servers.
  2. 2
    Your data stays local Favorites, preferences, search history, and alerts live on your phone and disappear when you clear app data or uninstall.
  3. 3
    You control advertising consent Ads are served through Google AdMob only after the required consent choice. You can change it anytime in Settings → Privacy Options.
